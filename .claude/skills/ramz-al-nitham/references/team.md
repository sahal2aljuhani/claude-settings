# فريق رمز النظام

51 AI employees in 14 teams (snapshot from the dashboard data). Each has `id`, `name`, `role`, `years`, `level`, `skills`, `tasks`, optional `lead` and `attire`. The `id` never changes; renames change only `name` (e.g. `khaled` is now روان). Tasks were rewritten around «سمّ» on the owner's request.

Special roles: `sultan` writes the daily video script; `yousef` handles reminders and renewals; `suhail` leads HR; `lulwa` merges reports; `hisham` leads governance.

To change people, edit `ramz/org` → `org.teams[].members[]` (see dashboard-guide.md), or tell the owner to use «تعديل» on the employee.
